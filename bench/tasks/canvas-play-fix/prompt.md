This folder has a small browser game: game.html and game.js, served by server.py (`python server.py <port>`). A player says the arrow keys and the coin do not work right.

Run the game as a service and play it in the browser to find out what is wrong. The left and right arrow keys should move the paddle left and right, and clicking the gold coin should add a point to the score drawn on the canvas and move the coin somewhere else. Fix what is broken, then play it again to show that it works.
